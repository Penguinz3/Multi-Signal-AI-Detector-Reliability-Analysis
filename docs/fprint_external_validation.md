# Computers protocol amendment: external education validation

The operational `asap_aes` corpus is already the official ASAP 2.0 release.
It remains one of the eight preregistered primary corpora and is not counted
twice.

BAWE is added as an external educational validation target. It participates in
global RAID-to-evaluation deduplication, token-length admission, grouped
partitioning, repeated target-signature draws, forecast locking, and detector
scoring. Student ID is the grouping key and disciplinary group is the
stratification variable. Writers spanning multiple groups receive a deterministic
composite stratum. Because BAWE is never a source, it has no source-summary or
source-model partitions; 250 whole writers are reserved for its signature pool.

BAWE never supplies source FPRs, probe profiles, nested-CV quantities, or model
training records. Its forecasts use all eight primary corpora as sources. All
nine zero-score forecast locks must exist before any target-signature scoring,
and all nine privileged locks must exist before any target-test scoring.

The decisive success gate remains unchanged and uses only the original eight
leave-one-corpus-out folds. BAWE results are a separately labeled external
validation, reported descriptively overall and by discipline, genre family, and
study level, including false flags per 1,000 passages. They do not support
claims about student intent, misconduct, or individual guilt.

BAWE files and full text remain local and are not redistributed. Before
submission, notify the BAWE team and include the corpus's prescribed
acknowledgement.
